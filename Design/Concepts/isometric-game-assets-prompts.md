# Arte da versão isométrica

As duas imagens foram criadas com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, derivadas do conceito `idle-devops-isometric-v1.png`.

- Cenário: `Assets/_IdleDataCenter/Resources/Isometrico/DataCenter.png`
- Rack com transparência: `Assets/_IdleDataCenter/Resources/Isometrico/Rack.png`
- Drone e interface: desenhados pelo código do jogo.

## Cenário

Edit the supplied concept screenshot into a production background asset for its Unity game. Keep the exact same charming detailed Kairosoft-like isometric pixel art direction, colors, equipment style and pleasant clean lighting. Output ONLY the central data-center environment, in landscape 3:2 aspect ratio, filling the image. Remove ALL game UI: no top bar, no left menu, no right panel, no bottom buttons, no floating area labels, no visible text anywhere, no slogans. All those will be rendered live by the game. Keep the open roof cutaway isometric facility with the NOC and world map dashboards at upper left, compute racks at upper middle, storage upper right of center, research lab at far upper right, power at middle left, network at center, blue pipe cooling at middle right. Important: in the upper-middle COMPUTE area remove the entire rows of tall dark compute cabinets and leave their tiled floor clear; we will place live rack sprites there. Keep network and storage cabinets. Remove the yellow BUILD RACK sign, yellow footprint and the drone from the foreground; leave the foreground center tiled floor wide and empty to receive live objects. Keep some stationary seated NOC operators and laboratory researcher, a helmeted power technician, delivery boxes and trolleys near the lower left, plants around the facility and subtle city backdrop. No hovering drones or walking workers; those are separate animated sprites in game. Precise crisp authentic pixel clusters, no blur, no photographic textures. Cohesive isometric 2:1 projection, straight clean grid, readable 7 zones, no UI or labels, opaque background.

## Rack

Create a single production game sprite asset to place into the supplied isometric data center reference. ONE standalone server rack cabinet, fully isolated on a genuinely transparent background, no floor, no backdrop, no text, no other objects, no shadow outside cabinet footprint. Match the Kairosoft-like detailed pixel art in the reference exactly: cute miniature crisp dark navy/slate tall rack, metallic blue-gray top, bevels, seven horizontal server drawers, tiny green/cyan and amber LEDs, vents. Exact isometric 2:1 view, visible top and two sides; front face points toward lower left (same orientation as the tall storage/server racks near center-right of the reference); base corners form an isometric diamond. Full rack from top to feet visible, centered, tight framing with only 5% transparent margin. Rack proportions: about twice as tall as wide. Authentic pixel art at approximately 48 by 80 logical pixels, clean nearest-neighbor enlarged pixels, no smooth 3D shading, no gradients, no anti-alias blur. Transparent PNG.
