## Título

Idle Data Center: an idle IT career game that lives above your taskbar

## Corpo

Hi! I just released my first game, **Idle Data Center**, a cozy idle game about a career in IT.

You start as a freelancer fixing the neighborhood's PCs in your bedroom. One day an IT company hires you, and from there you climb role by role: Technician, Sysadmin, Infrastructure Analyst, DevOps, SRE, Architect, CTO... until the IPO makes you CEO of a global cloud.

**What makes it different:** the game lives in a small strip just above the Windows taskbar. It keeps earning while you work, study or browse, and you open the full view whenever you want to check on your data center. You can drag the strip anywhere on the screen.

**Features**

- 🏢 **A data center that grows with you**: bedroom, office, server room, rack room, data center, campus and the world map, all in pixel art.
- 🎫 **Tickets with faces**: coworkers and clients ask for help; click and the tech walks over to fix it.
- ⌨️ **A real terminal**: press F1 and fix incidents with real commands like `systemctl restart named` and `kubectl rollout undo`. (It's always DNS.)
- 📈 **Clients, uptime and SLAs**: keep the sites up and your clients grow.
- 🏆 **Achievements and prestige**: sell the company and start over with certifications.
- 📖 **No IT background needed**: a built-in glossary explains every term, and underlined words explain themselves when you hover over them.
- 🌎 **English and Portuguese**.

**Price:** pay what you want. Download it for free, or leave a tip if you enjoy it.
**Platform:** Windows.
**Status:** early access. I'm still working on it, so any feedback, bug report or idea is very welcome in the comments!

👉 https://scaff94.itch.io/idle-data-center

Thanks for reading, and may your servers never go down. 🖥️
